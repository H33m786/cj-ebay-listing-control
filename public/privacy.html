Privacy Policy

CJ to eBay Listing Control is a private tool for preparing supplier products as eBay listing drafts.

When you connect an eBay sandbox or seller account, the app requests permission to read account policies, manage inventory listings, and read fulfilment information needed for listing and order workflows.

OAuth tokens and API credentials are intended to be stored locally or in private server-side configuration. They should not be shared publicly, pasted into chat, or placed in browser-visible code.

The app does not sell personal data. Data accessed from eBay is used only to operate listing, inventory, fulfilment, and account-policy features requested by the account owner.

Contact: use the owner contact details associated with the eBay developer account for this private application.
